📖 How to play
  • Place your five ships: Carrier (5), Battleship (4), Cruiser (3), Submarine (3) and Destroyer (2). Tap a ship in the shipyard, then a square. Tap a placed ship to rotate it, or drag it. While carrying a ship, R turns it.
  • Classic: one shot per turn. Salvo: one shot for every ship you still have afloat, so losing ships hurts twice.
  • Rookie fires half at random. Captain hunts properly once it hits. Genius computes where every remaining ship could fit.
🏅 Stats and badges

Fire by tapping or clicking enemy waters. Keyboard: Tab to a square, Enter to fire, R rotates while placing, N starts over.
