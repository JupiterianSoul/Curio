★ New personal best!

Your ship drifts: there is no friction worth mentioning, so thrust in short bursts and turn to aim. Big rocks split into two medium rocks, which split into two small ones. Clear every rock to finish a wave.

Rocks

  • Stone: the classic.
  • Ice: drops twice the crystals.
  • Metal: takes several hits per piece.
  • Volatile: explodes and damages everything nearby, including you.
  • Gold: rare, tough, full of crystals.

Crystals and the hangar

Crystals spill out of broken rocks and saucers and fade after a few seconds. Between waves you visit the hangar to spend them on rapid fire, spread shot, engines, shield cells, a crystal magnet, piercing rounds, smart bombs and spare ships.

Enemies and bosses

Saucers shoot at you (the small ones aim). Orange hunter drones chase you down. Every fifth wave brings a boss: the Mothership, the Rock Titan and the Hive Queen, in that order, then again but tougher.

Pickups

Rare floating capsules: S recharges a shield cell, B adds a smart bomb, O gives eight seconds of overdrive fire.

Controls

← → or A D turn, ↑ or W thrust, Space fire (hold for auto), H or Shift hyperspace, B smart bomb, P pause. On a phone use the buttons under the screen and turn on auto-fire.
