Level 1

  • Classic: some tiles flash. Click every one of them back, in any order.
  • Sequence: tiles flash one at a time. Click them in the same order. One wrong tile costs a life.
  • Mirror: click the reflection of each lit tile across the dashed middle line.
  • Daily: Classic rules with a pattern seeded by today's date, so you can compare with friends.
  • Three wrong tiles in a level costs a life. You have three lives. Levels grow the grid from 3x3 up to 7x7.
  • Keys: arrows move, Enter or Space picks, Esc returns to the menu.
