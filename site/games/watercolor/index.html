Ultramarinegranulating

Wet paint touching wet paint mixes and blooms. Edges darken as they dry. Blues and earths granulate into the paper grain. Clean water lifts paint back off. Autosaves when dry.

Keys: 1 to = pigments, M mix, W clean water, D hair dryer, [ ] size, U undo. On a touchpad, turn on Touchpad mode in the top bar: click to put the brush down, click again to lift it.
