Arrows or WASD to slide, swipe on a phone · U undo · H hint · N new game

Every move slides all tiles as far as they go in one direction. Two tiles with the same number that bump into each other merge into one with double the value, and that value is added to your score. After each move a new 2 (or sometimes a 4) appears. When the board is full and nothing can merge, the game is over.

Modes

  • Classic: reach 2048, then keep going as long as you can.
  • Goal: pick your own target tile, from a quick 64 up to a heroic 8192, and race to it in as few moves as possible.
  • Blitz: two minutes on the clock. Every merge counts. The timer starts on your first move.
  • Rocks: a couple of boulders sit on the board. They never move and never merge, so you have to work around them.
  • Daily: one 4×4 board for everyone today, same starting tiles and same random stream, no undo.

Tips

  • Pick a corner and keep your biggest tile in it.
  • Try to use only two or three directions most of the time.
  • Build a chain of descending tiles along one edge so merges cascade.
  • Stuck? The hint button runs a small look-ahead search and suggests a move.
