Tower of Hanoi

Move the whole tower to the goal peg. One disc at a time, and never a big disc on a smaller one. Legend says monks are doing this with 64 discs, and the world ends when they finish.

Discs

How to play

  1. Pick up the top disc of any peg and drop it on another peg.
  2. A disc can only land on an empty peg or on a bigger disc.
  3. Rebuild the whole tower on the goal peg, glowing green.

4 pegs gives you an extra peg, so the tower moves in far fewer moves. Clockwise only lets discs travel one step to the right, wrapping from the last peg back to the first. Scramble drops the discs in a random legal pile and asks you to tidy up.

Progress, stats and badges

Drag a disc, or tap a peg then tap where it should go. On a touchpad, Touchpad mode in the top bar lets you click a disc, glide, and click to drop. Keyboard: 1 to 4 pick up and drop, or ← → and Space. Z undo, H hint, R restart.
