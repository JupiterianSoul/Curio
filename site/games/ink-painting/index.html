Ink Painting

Load the brush, breathe out, commit to one stroke. Slow pools the ink, fast flicks go dry and wispy.

Tap the paper to stamp your seal
Ink on the brush 100%
Red seal your initials

A stylus paints with real pressure. Hold the brush still and the ink blooms. D dips, [ ] resize, S seal, U or Ctrl+Z undoes. On a touchpad, turn on Touchpad mode in the top bar: click to put the brush down, click again to lift it. Your painting autosaves.
