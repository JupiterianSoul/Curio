    How to play

    ← → Move
    ↓ Soft drop
    Space Hard drop
    ↑ X Rotate right
    Z Rotate left
    A Rotate 180
    C Shift Hold
    P Pause, R Restart

    On a phone: drag the board to move, tap to rotate (left half turns left), flick down to hard drop, flick up to hold. Or use the buttons.
