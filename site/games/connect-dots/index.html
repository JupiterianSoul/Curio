Drag from a dot to draw. On a touchpad, switch on Touchpad mode in the top bar: click a dot, glide, click again to let go. Drag over another pipe to cut it, drag back to retract. Tap the middle of a pipe to continue from there. Keyboard: arrows move, Space grabs and lets go, Z undo, R restart, H hint. A move counts each time you switch to a different colour, so a perfect game uses one move per colour.
