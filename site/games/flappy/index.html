    How to play

    • Tap, click, or press Space / ↑ / W to flap.
    • Fly through the gaps. Each pipe you pass is a point. Touch a pipe or the ground and it's over.
    • Coins are saved forever. Spend them in the shop on new flyers, worlds and trails.
    • Medals: bronze 10, silver 20, gold 40, platinum 80, diamond 150.
    • Squeeze close past a pipe edge for a "close shave" bonus coin.
    • P pauses, R restarts after a crash.
