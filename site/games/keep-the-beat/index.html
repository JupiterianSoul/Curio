120 BPM

Tap with a click, a finger, Space or Enter. Sound on helps a lot.

🥁

  • Hold the Tempo: the metronome clicks 8 times. Tap along. Then it goes quiet and you keep going for 16 (or 32) more beats. We measure how early or late every tap was, and whether you rushed or dragged.
  • Copy the Rhythm: one bar of count-in, then the drum plays a groove. Another count-in, then it is your turn: tap the same rhythm while the metronome keeps ticking. Eight grooves, from plain quarter notes to clave and habanera.
  • Guess the BPM: listen to 8 clicks and set the slider to the tempo. Five rounds. Musicians often know 120 by heart.
  • Keys: Space or Enter taps. Looking away pauses. Use headphones if your speakers lag.
