Beat Maker

Tap squares to light them up. Press the big Play button. That is a beat.

A 12 track drum machine with bass line, chords, effects, songs and a beat matching challenge.

  1. 1Click the squares. Each row is a drum, each square is a moment in time. Lit squares play.
  2. 2Press Play (or the Space bar). The light runs left to right and plays what is lit.
  3. 3Try a preset or press Make a beat. Change the drum kit and the tempo to taste.

Made a mess? Clear empties the grid, Reset brings back the starter beat, and Undo takes back any change. Want bass lines, effects and songs? Switch to Advanced in the top bar.
