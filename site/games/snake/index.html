    How to play

    • Steer with ← ↑ → ↓ or WASD. On a phone, swipe on the board or use the pad.
    • Eat apples quickly in a row to build a combo, up to x5 points.
    • Hit a wall, the edge or your own tail and it's over (unless you have a shield).
    • Glowing rings are portals: go in one, pop out of its twin.
    • P or Esc pauses, Space starts, R restarts.
