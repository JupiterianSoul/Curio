Dots and Boxes

Draw a line. Close a box and you go again. Never, ever hand over the third side.

📖 How to play and strategy
  • Take turns drawing one line between two neighbouring dots.
  • Complete the fourth side of a box to claim it, then you must draw again.
  • A box with three sides is a gift to whoever moves next. Turn on the warning to see risky lines in red dashes.
  • Late in the game the board becomes long chains. Whoever is forced to open the first long one usually loses.
  • The double-cross: when taking a chain, decline the last two boxes so your opponent has to open the next chain. The Sneaky AI knows this trick.
  • Keys: Tab to a line, Enter draws it, H hint, U undo, N new game.
