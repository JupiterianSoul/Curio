Keep the ball in play and break every brick to clear a level. Where the ball hits the paddle decides its angle: the edges send it flying sideways. Break bricks in a row without touching the paddle to build a combo that multiplies your points.

Bricks

  • Coloured bricks take 1 to 4 hits (the dots show how many).
  • Steel bricks never break. Bounce around them.
  • TNT bricks explode and take their neighbours with them. Chains are encouraged.
  • Gold bricks are worth 500 and always drop a power-up. ? bricks always drop one too.
  • Ghost bricks are invisible until you hit them. Green + bricks heal if you leave them half broken.

Bosses

Every fifth level a boss shows up with a health bar. Hit it with the ball or lasers. Its bombs and beams cannot kill you, but they zap your paddle so it freezes for a moment.

Controls

Mouse or finger anywhere on the board steers. ← → or A D work too. Click, tap, Space or ↑ launches. P or Esc pauses.
