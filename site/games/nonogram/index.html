Drag across a row or column to fill many squares at once, starting on a filled square erases instead. On a touchpad, Touchpad mode in the top bar lets you click, glide along a line, and click again. Cross mode (or right-click, or X) marks squares you know are empty. Keyboard: arrows, F or Space fill, X cross, Z undo. Every hand-made puzzle can be solved by pure logic, no guessing.
