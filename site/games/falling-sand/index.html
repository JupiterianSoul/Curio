200 elements, 300 reactions, one tiny universe. Pour, melt, grow, cook, zap and blow things up.

Keys: Space pause, . step, [ ] brush, R replace, H heat view, / search, E eraser on and off, I pick the element under the pointer. Right-click also erases. Touchpad: turn on Touchpad mode in the top bar, then click to start painting and click again to stop. Try: lava into the sea, a seed on dirt, popcorn on a heater, a mint in soda.
