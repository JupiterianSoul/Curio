📖 How to play

Place a disc so a straight line of your opponent's discs is sandwiched between it and another of yours. Every sandwiched disc flips. No legal move? You pass. Most discs when the board fills wins. Hover or Tab onto a dot to preview which discs would flip. Keys: arrows move, Enter plays, B best move, U undo, N new.
