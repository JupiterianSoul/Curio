Click or tap to flip. Arrow keys move between cards, Enter flips, N new game. Back-to-back matches build a combo.

📖 How to play
  • Flip two cards per turn. A match stays face up.
  • Pair themes (Capitals, Elements, Times tables, Spanish) match two different cards that belong together: France with Paris, Fe with Iron, 6 × 7 with 42.
  • Countdown: find every pair before time runs out. Each match adds two seconds.
  • vs Robot: take turns with a robot whose memory depends on its skill. A match earns another turn.
  • Stars: three for at most 1.6 moves per pair, two for 2.3.
