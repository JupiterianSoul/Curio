Your dinosaur runs by itself and keeps getting faster. Jump over things on the ground, duck under things in the air, and see how far you get. Every 600 m the world changes.

Five biomes

  • Dusty Desert: cacti and bouncing tumbleweeds.
  • Steamy Jungle: logs, thorn bushes and hanging vines you must duck under.
  • Frozen Tundra: ice spikes and runaway snowballs that roll faster than you.
  • Lava Lands: lava pits to leap, boulders and meteors (watch for their shadow).
  • Moonlit Plains: cacti, swooping bats and lots of pterodactyls.

Power-ups

  • Shield takes one hit for you, even a lava pit.
  • Magnet pulls in nearby amber. Amber x2 doubles every pickup.
  • Double jump lets you jump again in mid air.
  • Slow-mo slows the whole world down for a few seconds.
  • Rocket: blast forward and smash straight through obstacles.

Amber and friends

Amber you collect goes into your bank. Spend it to hatch five more runners, each with a perk: higher jumps, a built-in magnet, permanent double jump, a free shield, or longer power-ups.

Controls

Space, ↑ or W to jump (hold for a higher jump), ↓ or S to duck or drop faster. On a phone tap to jump and swipe down to duck, or use the big buttons.
