How to play

In Pictures, start dark and switch lights on until the board matches the drawing. Pressing a light twice undoes it, and order never matters, so every solution is just a set of lights to press.

Stats and badges

Tap a light, or use arrows and Space. Z undo, R restart, H hint, N next level.
