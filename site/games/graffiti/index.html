Hold still to make it drip. A stylus sprays harder when you press harder. 1 2 3 caps, S spray, M marker, T stencil (arrows move it, L lifts it), C next colour, N night, U or Ctrl+Z undo. On a touchpad, turn on Touchpad mode in the top bar: click to start spraying, click again to stop. Your wall autosaves.
