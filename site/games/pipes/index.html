Tap a tile to turn it clockwise. Switch on Turn back (or right-click, or press Q) to turn tiles the other way. Long-press or use lock mode to pin tiles you are sure of. Leaky open ends drip. In wrap-around mode pipes can leave one edge and come back on the other. Keyboard: arrows, Space rotate, Q rotate back, L lock, Z undo, H hint.
