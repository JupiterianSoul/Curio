How to plumb

  1. Tap a tile to turn it a quarter clockwise.
  2. Water starts at the blue pump. Every pipe end has to meet another pipe end.
  3. When every plant drinks and nothing leaks, the job is done.
  4. Turn back spins tiles the other way. Lock pins tiles you are sure of. Hint fixes one tile.
