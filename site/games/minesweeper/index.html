Building a fair board...

Click to dig · right-click, long-press or the flag tool to flag · click a satisfied number to open its neighbours · keys: arrows move, Enter dig, F flag, H hint, N new

Each number tells you how many of the eight squares around it hide a mine. Use that to work out which squares are safe and which to flag. Open every safe square to win. Your first click never hits a mine.

Boards

  • Beginner 9×9 with 10 mines, Intermediate 16×16 with 40, Expert 16×30 with 99.
  • Custom: any size up to 30×40 and as many mines as you dare.
  • Daily: one intermediate board for everyone today. Start on the glowing square.

No-guess mode

With no-guess switched on, boards are generated and checked by a solver so that every one can be cleared with pure logic. No coin flips, no bad luck, just thinking.

Scoring

Your time is the score. On a win you also see the board's 3BV (the minimum number of clicks needed to clear it) and your 3BV/s, which is how fast you really were. Hints cost 15 seconds each.

Useful patterns

  • A 1 that touches only one hidden square: that square is a mine.
  • 1-2-1 along a wall: the mines are under the 1s.
  • 1-2-2-1 along a wall: the mines are under the 2s.
  • Click a number whose mines are all flagged to open the rest of its neighbours in one go.
