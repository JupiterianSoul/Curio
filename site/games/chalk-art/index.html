Sidewalk Chalk

Warm pavement, a box of pastels and nowhere to be. The cracks will fight you. The rain always wins.

Chalk box Chalk White

Press lightly for dusty strokes, harder for solid colour (a stylus feels it, fast strokes skip more). Smudge blends colours together. Your pavement autosaves.

Keys: 1 to 0 colours, C chalk, S smudge, W sponge, [ ] size, R rain, U undo. On a touchpad, turn on Touchpad mode in the top bar: click to put the chalk down, click again to lift it.
