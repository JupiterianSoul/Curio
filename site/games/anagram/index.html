Tap the tiles or just type. Words get longer as you go.

Type letters, Backspace to undo, Space to shuffle, ? hint, Enter pass

  • Click tiles (or type) to place them on the rack. Click a placed tile to send it back.
  • Points: longer words are worth more, each tile's letter value adds a bonus, and three in a row starts a streak bonus. Hints cost a chunk of the word's value.
  • Sprint: 60 seconds, words grow from short to long. Themes: every word belongs to the theme you pick. Zen: no clock, just you and the tiles. Daily 10: the same ten words for everyone, against the stopwatch. Passing adds 20 seconds.
  • Any real word using all the tiles counts, so LEMON and MELON are both fine.
